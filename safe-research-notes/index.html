Safe Research Notes

Write a minimal note that keeps only public sources and non-sensitive remarks.

Smaller is safer

A note that carries more than it needs is a note that can leak. Keep only what the product review actually requires.

Keep in the note

  • The public link or the category name.
  • A non-sensitive check remark.
  • A short observation about the page.

Leave out

  • Any account or payment detail.
  • Any address, phone, or identity information.
  • Anything the review does not need.

A worked note

  1. Link: a public product link.
  2. Remark: a non-sensitive size question.
  3. Left out: login, address, and payment detail.
