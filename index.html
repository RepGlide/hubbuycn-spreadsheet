What Is Usually Safe to Share?

The Public Side of a Row

✓Usually safe

  • A product link that opens a public page.
  • The category and the product name.
  • A check question about size or color.
  • A note about what the page shows.

✕Keep private

  • Account login or credentials.
  • Payment method or amount.
  • Shipping address or phone.
  • Order numbers and support messages.
A Minimal Research Note

Only What the Review Needs

A good note keeps the public clue and the non-sensitive remark — nothing more.

1Keep in the note

  • The public link or category.
  • A non-sensitive check remark.
  • A short observation about the page.

2Leave out

  • Any account or payment detail.
  • Any address, phone, or identity info.
  • Anything the review does not need.

Screenshots Can Reveal More Than the Crop

Check the Whole Frame

A crop hides only part of the picture. The rest of the frame can still carry private detail.

    Hidden labels. A column or row header may reveal a name or address.

    Comments and history. Edits and notes can expose order or identity detail.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HubBuyCN spreadsheet?

A HubBuyCN spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a public reference to research, not a private account or order workspace.

Is this website the official HubBuyCN service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by HubBuyCN or Google.

Does this site store my account or order details?

No. This website does not collect or store any user input, and does not provide upload, login, order tracking, or automatic privacy scanning.

What should stay private?

Account credentials, payment details, full addresses, private orders, support conversations, and identity-related information should stay in a private account or personal record.

What is usually safe to share?

Public product links, category names, non-sensitive check questions, and observations about a public page are usually safe to share.

Where do I place an order?

After you have researched the public links, you continue on an external page to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders or store account details.
