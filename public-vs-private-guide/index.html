Public vs Private Guide

Separate shareable research clues from account, payment, address, order, and identity details.

Two spaces, one rule

A public clue and a private detail look alike until they leak into the same note. Keep them in separate spaces so one never drags the other along.

Public research

  • Public product links and category names.
  • Non-sensitive check questions.
  • Observations about a public page.

Private folio

  • Account credentials and payment details.
  • Full addresses and private orders.
  • Support conversations and identity-related information.
public stays public · private stays private
?Open item: has any private detail slipped into a public note?

A worked split

  1. Public: the link and category go into the research note.
  2. Private: the login and address stay in the account.
  3. Result: sharing the note shares only the public side.
